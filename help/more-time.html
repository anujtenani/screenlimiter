Getting more time

Last updated November 2026

The shield is the one screen you cannot avoid, so it is worth knowing exactly what each button costs before you tap it at a bad moment.

What the shield is telling you

When a group's minutes are gone, iOS draws the block screen over the app. It is made of four things:

  • The app's name and a title: "Instagram can wait".
  • An honest recap: "You used this hour's 5 minutes." If you wrote your own line in the group editor, that replaces the recap — but never the countdown.
  • The one promise that matters: "More time arrives at 9:00 PM."
  • The buttons, which depend on the group's strictness.

What each mode costs

Easy"Use it anyway" clears the shieldUnlimited One tap
Normal"Ask for more time" → the selector, or the app 53-second hold
HardOne stop, and that is the day1 8-second hold

Stops are counted per group, per calendar day. Spending them on a messaging group does not touch the video group's budget.

On iOS 26.4 and later, the shield can hand out the minutes itself

For Normal groups, tapping Ask for more time opens a small selector right on the shield: +2 min, +5 min, +10 min. Choosing one spends a stop, lifts the shield immediately and lets you carry on in the app you were already in — no Screen Limiter launch, no tab to find again.

Older iOS versions, Easy and Hard keep the previous shape on purpose. Easy grants in one tap, and Hard's long hold is the entire point of Hard. On those versions, asking opens Screen Limiter, where the same grant happens behind a hold.

Extra minutes do not move the window. A grant lasts for the chosen length or until the current window ends, whichever comes first. If the hour turns two minutes after you ask, you get two minutes and then the new allowance — not a bonus hour.

When the stops run out

The primary button changes to "Out of stops today" and there is nothing left to press but Okay. The group stays blocked until the next window, at which point a normal allowance returns and the stops reset at midnight. This is not a bug to work around; a mode with a daily cap is only meaningful if the cap holds.

Why asking sometimes fails instantly

  • "The hour is about to turn — your allowance is back in a moment." The window boundary is too close for a meaningful grant. Wait for it.
  • "You have used all 5 stops for today." The cap has been reached.
  • The shield flashes and returns. iOS refused to schedule the grant; the shield is reapplied so a failed request never leaves an app quietly unblocked.

Making the shield less necessary

Most people who fight the shield are fighting the allowance, not the friction. If five minutes an hour is genuinely not enough for a group, raise it rather than spending four stops by mid-morning — a limit you negotiate with constantly is the one that gets deleted. Ten minutes for the group you actually need, five for the one you don't.

Questions

Does asking for more time let me use the app for that many minutes?

Yes, up to the end of the current window. The shield comes back when the grant runs out, and the next window's allowance is unaffected.

Do the stops reset at midnight or at the next window?

Stops reset at the start of each calendar day. The allowance resets at every window boundary, which is a different clock.

Can I turn the shield off entirely for a trip?

Yes — pause all limits from the Limits screen, or switch one group off. Both are deliberate actions with a visible state, and Siri can pause and unpause them for you.

Why is the +2 / +5 / +10 selector missing?

It requires iOS 26.4 or later, and only appears on Normal groups that still have stops left. On any other configuration the button opens Screen Limiter instead.
