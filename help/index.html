Quick answers

Nothing is blocked at all

Screen Time access is almost always the reason. iOS asks once, and if the prompt was declined the app cannot ask again — see Screen Time access.

It works, then stops working after a day

Usually Screen Time itself was switched off in Settings, or the app's access was revoked. Screen Limiter shows a banner when it notices. Settings → Screen Time → Apps With Screen Time Access is where that switch lives.

The blocked app isn't the one I picked

Picker selections can go stale if the app was deleted, reinstalled or updated its bundle. Screen Limiter lifts a shield it can no longer justify rather than leaving an app blocked with no way out — re-pick the apps in the group to arm it again.

My group is blocked but the timer says the window just reset

That is the design working. The shield lifts at the window boundary, the new allowance is immediately available, and the group blocks again as soon as it is spent.

Settings are locked and I don't have the code

Parent Mode is on. Every change needs the code its owner set, and the security question is the only way back. Passcode and Parent Mode has the details.

Where is my data?

On this phone, in the app's own storage. There is no account, no server and no analytics — which also means there is nothing to recover if the app is deleted.

Before you email

Two minutes of detail makes a problem findable, because there is no telemetry on our side to look at:

  • Your iOS version (Settings → General → About) and your device.
  • The group's allowance, window, strictness, active days and hours.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot of Settings → Diagnostics in Screen Limiter. It shows the authorization state, whether an activity is armed and when the monitor last woke up, which separates "iOS refused" from "the limit never fired".

Email support at hello@screenlimiter.com. There is no ticket system — a person reads it.
