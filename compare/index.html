How Screen Limiter compares

Last updated November 2026

Eight apps are worth knowing about in this category, and there is no winner, because the right one depends on what your phone time actually looks like. Here is the map: what shape of limit each app uses, and a page for every comparison that matters.

Read this first. Screen Limiter did not invent the hourly allowance. Precursor shipped the same mechanic first — small allowance, resets at the top of the hour, three strictness levels, no account — and if you want that and nothing else, it is a fair choice. Screen Limiter exists because we wanted a different set of windows and no analytics at all. The full comparison is here.

How these pages are written

Every claim about another app comes from that app's own App Store listing, website or help docs, and the working notes are dated. Prices are described as tiers rather than numbers, because three of the six apps currently contradict their own pricing somewhere, and every number goes stale. Where something could not be verified, the page says so instead of guessing.

No app here is bad. Each one is a reasonable answer to a slightly different question, and the useful move is to work out which question you have.
