If you are not sure where to start

Start with the shape of the problem rather than the app. One daily budget, spent by 9:40am, is the failure most people are living with, and it is a design problem rather than a discipline problem. Why daily limits fail explains the mechanism; how Screen Limiter works is the alternative in five steps.

If your problem is a specific app rather than a specific hour, the two guides above on Shorts and Reels are the honest ones: neither can be blocked on its own, and any page that tells you otherwise is describing a different product.
